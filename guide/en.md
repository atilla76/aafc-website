
# Klirena User Guide

·

## Getting started

Klirena works on your phone (iPhone, Android) and on your computer (klirena.com/panel) with the same account. Data entered in one place appears in the other immediately.

### Installation and registration

1. Download the app from the App Store or Google Play by searching for "Klirena".
2. Tap **Register**. Enter your e-mail and password, and accept the terms of use and the privacy policy.
3. Click the confirmation link sent to your e-mail. You cannot sign in with an unconfirmed account. If the e-mail does not arrive, check your spam folder. The sender is support@klirena.com.
4. Sign in. If you forget your password, tap **Forgot Password?** on the sign-in screen to request a reset e-mail.

### Setup wizard

On your first sign-in, a setup card appears on the home screen. You can also open it later from Settings > Setup wizard. The steps are:

1. **Clinic info and logo:** clinic name, doctor name, address, phone, social media accounts. The logo appears on PDF reports, collages and pages sent to patients.
2. **First patient:** a name and a phone number are enough.
3. **First photo:** a visit photo with the angle guide.
4. **Reminder message:** edit your appointment reminder text.
5. **Online booking:** open the page where patients book their own appointments.
6. **Team:** invite an assistant or a second doctor.

You can skip steps and come back later. Completed steps stay marked.

### How your clinic works

In Clinic Info, choose how your clinic works. The screens are simplified accordingly.

| Option | Who it is for | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Aesthetic | Clinics that only perform procedures | A new visit opens as Procedure |
| Outpatient | Practices that only see consultations | A new visit opens as Consultation. On the patient page, packages, the health form and the before-after comparison are hidden |
| Mixed | Those who do both | A new visit opens with the type you used last |

You can change it in the app under Settings > Clinic Info, and in the panel under Settings > Clinic Info. When you open a procedure or check-up visit for a patient, the hidden sections appear again for that patient.

### Users and roles

Go to Settings > Users > Add User, enter an e-mail and choose a role to add a team member. A clinic can have up to 5 users.

| Role | What they see and do |
|---|---|
| Admin | Everything: accounting, users, settings |
| User (assistant, doctor) | Patients, visits, photos, appointments, consents; cannot see accounting |

### Language

Choose from 10 languages in Settings > Language. Messages sent to patients, consent pages, the booking page and the patient portal are also prepared in this language.

### Subscription

The plans are shown on the Premium card at the top of Settings. The subscription is purchased, managed and cancelled through your App Store or Google Play account. Klirena does not store payment details.

## Patients

Everything starts from the patient card: visits, photos, consents, appointments, packages and account are gathered on one screen.

### Adding a patient

1. On the home screen, tap **Add Patient** or the + button at the bottom.
2. Enter the full name and phone number; this is enough. The phone number is saved in a single format with the country code, and WhatsApp messages go to this number.
3. If you wish, add date of birth, gender, e-mail, nationality and a note.

### Searching and listing

On the Patients screen, search by name or phone. The list is sorted by last visit. In the panel (PATIENTS tab) the same list opens as a wider table.

### Patient details

| Section | What it contains |
|---|---|
| Info | Identity, contact, notes; change them with the edit icon |
| Visits | All visits in date order, with the number of procedures and photos |
| Consents | Signed and pending consents, their PDFs |
| Appointments | Past and upcoming appointments |
| Packages | Purchased packages, remaining sessions |
| Account | Fees, payments, remaining balance (admin only) |
| Documents | Uploaded lab results, discharge summaries, PDFs and images |

Shortcuts in the patient details: write on WhatsApp, call, send consent, share photos, compare (collage), patient portal link, health form.

### Patient portal

The patient portal is a page where the patient sees their appointments, packages, shared photos and consents through a single link. Send the link from the patient details; the patient signs in with the last 4 digits of their phone number.

### Health (intake) form

Send the form by WhatsApp from an appointment or visit; it can also be requested automatically after an online booking. The patient answers questions such as allergies, medications, pregnancy and past procedures on their own phone. Risky answers appear as a warning on the home screen and are kept on the patient card.

### Deleting and merging patients

A patient is deleted from the menu in the patient details. The patient is removed together with their visits, photos and consents, so be sure first. During import, duplicate patients are skipped automatically.

## Visits and photos

A visit is each time the patient comes to the clinic. The date, area, procedures performed, photos, diagnosis, injection map and products used are kept in the visit.

### New visit

1. Open the patient and tap **Add Visit**.
2. Select the area: Face, Neck, Abdomen, Legs or Other.
3. Select the visit type: **Procedure**, **Control** or **Consultation**. For a control, you pick one of the earlier visits that has not been checked yet; the control is linked to that visit and the before-after comparison is matched automatically. For a consultation, only the date is chosen (see Consultation visit).
4. Select the date (today by default; a past date can be selected, a future date cannot).
5. Type the procedures performed or choose them from the list (Botox, Filler, Mesotherapy, Chemical peel, PRP, Skin rejuvenation…). Example: "3 areas Botox, lip filler".

### Guided photo capture (5 angles)

The camera asks for 5 angles in order: front, right 45°, left 45°, right profile, left profile.

- The photo from the previous visit appears faintly in the background; bring the patient to the same position and shoot.
- The tilt of the phone is measured; if the phone is not level, it warns you. This way the before-after photos are taken from the same angle.
- You can skip an angle and shoot it again later. You can also add a photo from the gallery.
- Photos are not saved to the phone gallery; they are saved encrypted to the clinic's cloud archive.

Tip: the same light, the same background and the same distance show the before-after difference best. Have the patient remove jewelry and makeup, and tie the hair back.

### Visit details

| Card | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Photos | Photos by angle; tap to enlarge, swipe to move between angles |
| Diagnosis (ICD-10) | Search by code or name (e.g. L81.1, melasma, D22.3 nevus). The ICD-10 list and aesthetic diagnoses are ready to use |
| Injection map | Mark points on the face diagram, enter product and amount |
| Treatment plan | Planned area and products for the next sessions |
| Products used | With lot / serial no; deducted from stock |
| Notes | Anamnesis, findings, recommendations |
| Fee | The visit's fee lines and payment (see the Fees section) |

If the product name you enter in the injection map is the same as the name in Stock, the amount is deducted from stock automatically.

### Consultation visit

A consultation visit is for appointments where no procedure is performed: complaints and findings are written, a prescription and lab tests are requested, and a consent is obtained if needed.

1. Open the patient, choose **Add Visit** → **Consultation**, enter the date and tap **Open consultation**. Area, favorites, photos and fee are not asked at this step.
2. On the consultation screen, fill in the following in order: chronic conditions, complaint, history, findings, result and recommendation.
3. **Prescription:** write one drug per line. **Share prescription** creates an A5 PDF with the clinic logo and the patient and diagnosis details; send it by WhatsApp, e-mail or printer. It is not an electronic prescription.
4. **Lab tests:** write the requested tests. When the result arrives, photograph it or attach its PDF; the file is also saved to the patient's documents.
5. **Consent:** if an interventional procedure will be performed, send the patient a consent with **Send Consent**; the consent is linked to this visit. You can also select and link a consent that was signed earlier.
6. Diagnosis and fee are filled in as in other visits. If a drug or vaccine was given, see the section below.

In the patient list and the agenda, these visits appear as **Consultation**; in the statistics they are counted on a separate line. If a consultation was opened by mistake, you can delete the visit.

### Drugs and vaccines given

An injection, vaccine or drug given to the patient is recorded on the **Drugs / vaccines given** card in the visit by selecting it from stock. This way it is deducted from stock and its lot stays recorded.

1. Select the product on the card or scan the box's barcode. For products defined in ml, the amount is asked in ml; for others, as a count.
2. In the window that opens, select the route: IM, SC, ID, IV, Local or Oral. Enter the site (e.g. left deltoid).
3. Mark the dose order: 1st, 2nd, 3rd dose or Booster. If you enter a next dose date, the patient appears on the **Recalls** list on that date and the reminder message is ready.
4. If the patient's card has an allergy record, a red warning appears above the card; check it before giving the drug. If no allergy is recorded, it shows a green "no allergy record".

In the panel, the same card is in the visit details under the heading **Drugs / vaccines given**.

### Before-after collage

At least 2 visits are required.

1. In the patient details, tap the compare button.
2. Select the visits (e.g. the procedure day and the 2-week check-up) and the angles.
3. In the collage editor, adjust the layout and the display of date and logo.
4. Save or share it as a collage.

### PDF report

In the visit details, tap **PDF report**. Layout options:

| Layout | When |
|---|---|
| One-page summary | All angles on 1 page; ideal for sharing |
| One page per angle | Large and detailed; for files and legal records |
| Compact | 2 angles per page |

The **with previous** option places it side by side with the previous visit. In the content, the procedures performed, age and date difference, and the patient's phone can be turned on or off. The report is prepared with the clinic logo and details.

### Sending photos to the patient

In the patient details, tap the share icon and select the photos. A time-limited, secure link is sent by WhatsApp; you can revoke the link at any time. The photo file is not attached to WhatsApp, so control stays with you.

### Documents

Upload PDFs and images such as lab results, discharge summaries and external reports to Documents in the patient details. You can also add them by drag and drop in the panel.

## Consents

The patient signs the consent on their own phone after verifying with a 6-digit code; the signed PDF is saved to the patient's file with a timestamp. No paper, scanner or filing cabinet is needed.

### Preparing templates

1. Open Settings > Consent Templates.
2. Add a language with the icon at the top right; 5 ready templates are loaded in the language you choose. You can add more than one language for your foreign patients.
3. Open a template and edit the text to fit your practice, or create a new template (Consent Name + Consent Content). The live preview shows what the patient will see.

Important: the ready templates are only starting texts and are not approved by a lawyer. Review them with your legal adviser before using them; responsibility for the content lies with the clinic owner.

### Sending a consent

1. In the patient details, tap **Send Consent** and select the template.
2. The message comes ready: the link and the 6-digit verification code. Send it by WhatsApp or copy the link. The code is valid for 24 hours.
3. If the patient is at the practice, they can open it on their own phone and sign there.

### Signing on the patient's side

1. The patient opens the link and enters the 6-digit code.
2. They read the consent text. The text is shown in the patient's language.
3. They enter their ID or passport number and tick the confirmation box.
4. They sign on the screen with a finger and tap Sign.

If a field is missing (signature, confirmation box, invalid ID number), the page says which one is missing.

### After signing

- The signed PDF is created with the text, signature, identity verification, date-time and timestamp, and is stored so that it cannot be altered. A backup copy is also kept separately.
- It appears under Consents in the patient details and under Settings > Sent Consents. Pending (unsigned) consents are also in this list.
- You can link the consent to the relevant visit (visit details > link consent). The link can be removed.
- You can revoke an unsigned consent; the link no longer works.

The patient also sees the consents they signed in the patient portal.

## Appointments and agenda

The appointment book prevents conflicts, sends reminders with one tap, and lets patients book their own appointments through online booking.

### Adding an appointment

1. On the home screen, tap **Add appointment** or the + button; or tap an empty time on the Appointments screen.
2. Select the patient (you can also add a new patient from here).
3. Select the service, duration, doctor and room/device.
4. Save. A second appointment cannot be given to the same doctor, room or device at the same time.

Tap an appointment to edit, move or cancel it, or mark it as "Arrived" / "No Show" (the no-show rate appears in the statistics). For a patient who arrived, the visit is opened from here.

### Doctors, rooms, blocked times

| Setting | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Doctors | Settings > Doctors | Each doctor can have their own working hours; they can be shown or hidden in online booking. Not needed in a single-doctor clinic |
| Rooms & devices | Settings > Rooms & devices | Prevents conflicts on a single device such as a laser or HIFU |
| Blocked times | Settings > Blocked times | Blocks a day or time range for surgery, leave or holidays; these times do not appear in online booking |

The appointment book can be filtered by doctor.

### Reminders

On the Appointments screen, tap an appointment and choose WhatsApp or SMS; the message comes ready with the patient name, date and time, and you only tap Send. Change the message text in Settings > Reminders; if the field is empty, the default text is used.

### Waiting list

Add a patient to the waiting list for a fully booked day. When an appointment is cancelled, send the patients on the list a "a slot has opened" message by WhatsApp with one tap.

### Online booking

1. Open it from Settings > Online booking.
2. Edit the services, durations and working hours in the panel under the ONLINE BOOKING tab.
3. Copy the link or download the QR code; add it to your Instagram bio, Google Business profile, WhatsApp Business and website. On Instagram: Edit profile > Links > Add external link.

The patient sees the free times, selects a service and (if any) a doctor, and enters their name and phone. The appointment appears in your appointment book with a "🌐 Online" label. If you wish, the health form is then requested automatically.

### Phone calendar

With Settings > Calendar subscription, add the appointments to your iPhone, Google or Outlook calendar. It is one-way (changes made in the calendar do not come to Klirena). For privacy, you can hide patient names.

### Agenda

In the panel, the AGENDA tab gives the daily and weekly view on a wide screen; it suits the reception computer.

### Recalls and Google reviews

The time for a check-up and repeat is calculated automatically by procedure (each procedure such as Botox, filler, mesotherapy and PRP has its own interval). Patients whose time has come are listed on the home screen and on the Recalls screen.

1. Tap the WhatsApp button next to the patient; the check-up or refresh message comes ready. You can edit the texts in Settings ([ad], [islem], [klinik] fields are filled in automatically).
2. Ask satisfied patients for a Google review: enter your Google review link once in Settings > Reminders, and the message comes ready with the link.

The statistics screen shows how many messages were sent and how many of those patients booked an appointment.

## Fees, packages and accounting

The fee is entered in the visit; collection, remaining balance, installments and package sessions are posted to the patient account and to accounting automatically. Only admin users see this section.

### Fee and payment in the visit

1. In the visit details, tap **Add fee**.
2. Enter the amount and description. If there is more than one procedure, add lines with **Split by procedure** (e.g. Consultation 1,500 + Plexr 8,000). A procedure name must be written on every line that has an amount.
3. Select the payment: In full, Partial or After. Payment method: Cash, Card, Bank transfer.
4. The partial amount or the amount left for later is written to the patient's account as a debt.
5. If product lot costs were entered for the products used in the visit, the visit card also shows the product cost and the profit.

Restrictions: the amount cannot be set lower than the payment received; a fee with installments is edited from the patient account.

### Patient account and installments

The Account card in the patient details shows all fees, payments and the open balance. When you tap Take payment, the amount is applied first to the next installment, or to the open balance if there is none. You set the number of installments when entering a fee or package; installments that fall due appear as a warning on the home screen.

### Packages

A package is sessions sold in advance (e.g. Mesotherapy, 5 sessions).

1. Patient details > Packages > Add package.
2. Enter the package name, number of sessions, optional expiry date, package fee and number of installments. If a fee is entered, it is added to the patient account as a debt.
3. At each session, tap "Use a package session" in the visit details. No separate fee is charged for that session; the card shows "[used] / [total] sessions".
4. A session deducted by mistake is corrected with Undo. If a package is cancelled, the sessions already used stay recorded.

Package statuses: active, Completed, Expired, Cancelled. The patient sees their remaining sessions in the patient portal.

### Accounting screen

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Summary | Income, expenses and net for the selected month; breakdown by category and payment method |
| Entries | All income and expense records; patient, date, amount |
| Receivable | Patients' open balances and overdue installments |
| By month | Income, expense and net month by month |

Enter non-visit income (product sales etc.) and expenses with the Income / expense button. Expense categories: Product purchase (stock), Rent, Staff / salary, Bills, Tax / social security, Marketing, Accounting / consulting, Device / equipment, Other.

### Closing the till

At the end of the day, tap Close the day; that day's cash collection is shown and marked as closed. Days that have not been closed are reminded on the home screen.

### Sending to the accountant

"Send to accountant (Excel/CSV)" gives all entries of the selected period as a file. It can also be downloaded in the panel from the ACCOUNTING tab.

## Stock

You enter stock by scanning the box barcode; the amount is deducted when used on a patient. You can always find which lot was given to which patient.

### Defining a product

Settings > Stock (STOCK tab in the panel) > Add product:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Product name | Write it with the same name in the injection map (e.g. "20u Botox"); the amount is deducted from stock automatically |
| Unit | Units (u) or ml |
| Box content | How many units / ml are in one box (e.g. 100 u toxin, 1.5 ml filler); multiplied by the number of boxes, and the cost is calculated accordingly |
| Minimum stock | A warning appears when stock falls below this amount |
| Use within (after opening) | In days; a warning appears when the opened vial's time has expired |
| Product barcode (GTIN) | Saved automatically on the first scan |

### Receiving stock (lot)

1. Tap Receive lot or Scan barcode / QR.
2. Bring the code on the box (DataMatrix) into the frame. The GTIN, lot, serial no and expiry date are read from the code.
3. If the barcode is scanned for the first time, select which product it is or create a new product; on later scans the product is recognized automatically.
4. Enter the quantity, unit cost and supplier. If the same lot already exists, the quantity is added to the existing lot.

### Daily use

- Add the product used in the visit (by selecting the lot) or write it in the injection map; stock is deducted and the lot is recorded on the patient's visit.
- When you open a vial, tap "Vial opened"; the time it stays open is tracked.
- Use Waste / disposal and Count adjustment (+ surplus, − shortage) to match stock to reality.
- Alerts: Low stock, Expiring soon, Opened vial expired. They also appear on the home screen.
- "Patients who received this lot": if a product is recalled, all patients who received that lot are listed.

### Cost and profit

If a unit cost is entered when receiving a lot, the visit card and the profit report calculate the real material cost. Products without a box price show a "cost missing" warning in the report.

## Statistics and reports

The statistics screen (Statistics in the app, STATISTICS in the panel) shows how many patients came in the period you select, what each procedure earned, and whether recalls are working.

### Choosing a period

This Month, Last Month, Select month, This Year, Last Year, Last 12 months. In the panel, clicking a bar in the months chart opens that month. The percentage next to the figures is the change compared with the previous period.

### Indicators

| Indicator | Meaning |
|---|---|
| New patients | Patients registered for the first time in the period |
| Visits + controls | Procedure visits and control visits, separately |
| Online | Appointments from online booking |
| Appointments | Total, completed, cancelled and no-show rate |
| Recall conversion | Number of messages sent → number of patients who booked |
| Income | Collections of the period (admin) |
| Product consumption | Units / ml used, by product |

### Procedures list

For each procedure category, "N visits · X (M payments)" is shown: the number of visits, the income attributed to that procedure, and how many payments the income came from. The number of visits and the number of payments can differ; an installment or partial payment writes more than one payment to a single visit.

Consultation rule (for statistics only):

- In a fee such as "Consultation + Plexr", the consultation line is not counted separately; its income is added to the procedure (Plexr).
- A fee written only as "Consultation" is counted for the procedure if the visit's procedure is known.
- A payment not linked to a visit is linked to the same patient's visit on the same day.

This way the "Consultation" line shows only the patients who really came just for a consultation. Accounting records do not change.

### Profit report

The admin sees, by procedure, income − material cost = profit. For the report to be correct, it lists the following gaps as warnings:

- [n] visits have no fee entered.
- [n] paid visits have no material entered; the cost is counted as 0.
- [n] visits have a product used whose box price is not entered; the cost is incomplete.

### Export

Accounting entries are downloaded as Excel/CSV, and all data from the BACKUP tab in the panel (see Web panel).

## Web panel

klirena.com/panel lets you use the whole app from a computer; it is the most comfortable place for reception, bulk work, import and backup. The data is identical to the app immediately.

### Signing in

Open klirena.com/panel in the browser of any computer and sign in with your e-mail and password from the app. On a shared computer, sign out when you are done. You can choose the panel language at the top right; the app's language is chosen in the app.

### Import (moving from an old program)

The patient list, visit history and photos can be moved from any program in one go.

1. From the old program, export the patient list and the procedure/visit list as Excel or CSV, and the photo folder as it is (or as a ZIP). The first row of the file must be the column headers; at most 20,000 visits per file.
2. Drop them all together on the IMPORT tab; which file is which is separated automatically.
3. Check the column mapping, then tap Preview and Start. The order is: patients first, then visits, photos last.
4. Patients who are in the visit list but not in the archive are created automatically. Link photo folders that do not match to a patient, or add them as a new patient.

Duplicates are skipped. Every import can be undone: the imported photos and the visits left empty, or the imported visits that have no photo/document added, are deleted. If you cannot export, write to support@klirena.com and we will do the move for you. Do not send patient data by e-mail.

### Secure upload link

If the files are with someone else (the old program's company, your assistant), create a link from SETTINGS > Secure upload link and send it. The other party uploads the files to this page and they come directly to your panel; no e-mail or flash drive is needed. The link is time-limited and can be revoked at any time.

### Backup and data download

Data is stored automatically and encrypted in the cloud and is also backed up every night; you do not need to make a backup. If you want your own copy, download it from the BACKUP tab:

| Format | Content |
|---|---|
| ZIP | All patients, visits, photos and documents in folders; recommended for a full backup |
| HTML | A report in which you can see the photos directly in the browser |
| JSON / CSV | Raw data for moving to another program |

In the app, Settings > Data Access leads to the same options.

## Security, data protection and support

Patient data is stored on a secure cloud server over an encrypted connection, each clinic sees only its own data, and it is backed up every night. What you need to do on your side is below.

### What you need to do

- Give every user their own account; do not share passwords. Remove staff who leave right away from Settings > Users.
- Choose a password of at least 8 characters that you do not use anywhere else; in the panel, SETTINGS > Change password.
- Keep the screen lock on your phone turned on.
- Send photos to the patient with a time-limited share link, not as a WhatsApp attachment.
- Do not send patient data by e-mail; use the secure upload link to receive files.
- When asking for support, cover the patient name and phone in the screenshot.
- Give your patient the data protection notice and obtain consent for procedures that require explicit consent (with the consent templates).

### Roles of the clinic and Klirena

Under data protection law, the clinic is the data controller and Klirena is the data processor. Klirena processes patient data only to provide the service and does not use it for advertising or any other purpose. Details are in the privacy policy at klirena.com.

### Deleted records

A deleted visit is removed from the lists together with its photos, documents and linked controls; the record is kept in the archive. Signed consents cannot be deleted.

### Account deletion

Send an e-mail from your registered e-mail address to support@klirena.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". The account and all clinic data are permanently deleted. Before that, download your data as a ZIP from the panel. Also cancel the subscription separately in the App Store or Google Play; deleting the account does not stop the subscription.

When reporting a problem, include the app version (at the very bottom of Settings), the phone model, what you were doing, and a screenshot with patient information covered.
