Frequently asked

Everything you need to know about Klirena.

Is my patient data secure?

Yes. All photos and patient records are encrypted in transit and at rest. Klirena stores data on Supabase secure infrastructure with row-level security, and runs automatic nightly backups to Cloudflare R2. We never share data with third parties or advertisers. You can export your full data anytime, and request account deletion via our deletion page.

How do I bring my data from my old program?

Patient list, visit history and photos move from any program in one go. 1) Export the patient list and the treatment/visit list from your old program as Excel or CSV, and the photo folder as it is (or a ZIP). 2) On a computer open klirena.com/panel > IMPORT and drop them all at once; each file is recognised automatically. 3) Press Preview and Start: patients first, then visits, then photos. Patients in the visit list who are not in your archive are created automatically; unmatched photo folders can be linked to a patient or added as new patients. Duplicates are skipped and every import can be undone. If you can't export, write to support@aafclinic.com and we'll move it for you.

Who is Klirena for?

Klirena is built for licensed medical professionals: plastic surgeons, dermatologists, ENT specialists, aesthetic doctors, and clinic teams. It is a documentation and archive tool; it does not provide diagnosis or treatment guidance.

How do WhatsApp reminders work?

Tap a patient's appointment, then tap the WhatsApp button — your customizable reminder message opens directly in WhatsApp with the patient's number pre-filled. Send in one tap. This uses WhatsApp's click-to-chat, so there is zero per-message cost. You can also send via SMS using your phone's native messaging.

Can patients book appointments online?

Yes. Turn on online booking, choose your services, durations and working hours, and share your link or QR code on Instagram, Google and your website. Booked times are blocked automatically, and patients can join a waiting list when a day is full.

Does it work for clinics with several doctors?

Yes. Add each doctor with their own working hours. Appointments are assigned to a doctor, the same doctor cannot be double-booked, and patients can choose a doctor — or "any doctor" — when booking online.

Can I record ICD-10 diagnoses?

Yes. Add one or more diagnoses to any visit by searching code or name. Klirena includes the full ICD-10 list, the official Turkish Ministry of Health list, and common aesthetic diagnoses in 10 languages.

Does the 14-day free trial auto-renew?

The trial converts to a paid 49.99 USD per month subscription unless you cancel before the trial ends. You can cancel anytime through your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Can I use Klirena with my team?

Yes. The Multi-User Clinic Access feature lets you invite up to 5 team members under your clinic account, each with role-based permissions.

What devices does Klirena support?

Klirena runs on iPhone (iOS 15+), iPad, Android phones and tablets (Android 7+), and Apple Silicon Macs. The web panel works in any browser for patients, appointments, accounting, online booking settings, backups and exports.

What languages are supported?

Klirena is fully localized in 10 languages: Turkish, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Korean, and Chinese — including reminder message templates per clinic.

Do you store photos of identifiable patients?

You decide. Klirena is an archive tool you control. We recommend obtaining patient consent before storing identifiable medical photos, and following your local medical data regulations (KVKK, GDPR, HIPAA, etc.).
