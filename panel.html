Doctors

For clinics with more than one doctor. The same doctor cannot get two appointments at the same time, and patients can choose a doctor in online booking. Unassigned appointments count as busy for every doctor.

If you assign a room or device to an appointment, it cannot be given to two appointments at the same time. You can filter the appointment book by room or device.
